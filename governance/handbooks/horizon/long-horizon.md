# Long-horizon mode

The terms **MUST**, **MUST NOT**, **SHOULD**, **SHOULD NOT**, and **MAY** use RFC 2119 meanings.

This handbook records the complete selectable mode definition. The recorded involvement profile and its snapshotted contract select the mode; the name grants no additional authority.

## Escalation ladder

| Identifier | Source                 | Kind          | What it decides                                                                                                 | Mark    | Reason                                                                    |
| ---------- | ---------------------- | ------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| LH-R1      | task failure signature | retry rung    | A transient signature may retry at most twice per task.                                                         | retry   | A changed signature can be transient, but the bound prevents looping.     |
| LH-R2      | task failure signature | strategy rung | A repeated signature or spent retry budget routes once to the declared repair strategy.                         | switch  | Repetition requires a different approach rather than the same submission. |
| LH-R3      | task failure record    | planning rung | A spent strategy rung may start one re-plan scoped to the failed task and its dependents.                       | re-plan | Structural failure may invalidate only that dependency subgraph.          |
| LH-R4      | task failure record    | terminal rung | A spent re-plan or human-only blocker defers the task and blocks its dependents while the collection continues. | defer   | Unrelated eligible work must not wait for one task.                       |
